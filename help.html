A new face, in a few clicks.

You need a CMF Watch Pro 2 and a Windows or Mac computer with Bluetooth. Open this website in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

  1. Choose your face. Pick any of the eight designs and select “Choose this face.”
  2. Release the phone connection. Temporarily turn off Bluetooth on the phone paired with your watch. Leave Bluetooth on your computer turned on.
  3. Connect your watch. Click “Connect watch,” choose your Watch Pro 2 in the browser window and connect.
  4. Choose what to replace. Select an existing face you are happy to replace. If space is available, you can choose “Add as a new face” instead.
  5. Install. Click “Install face.” Keep the watch nearby until the transfer finishes. Check the face on your watch, then disconnect here and turn your phone’s Bluetooth back on.

If the watch does not appear

Check that your computer’s Bluetooth is on, the watch is nearby, and the paired phone has released its connection. Close any other browser connection to the watch, then try again.

If the replacement list is empty

Disconnect and reconnect to reload the watch’s faces. If the watch is full, choose an existing face to replace.

If Connect watch is unavailable

Use Chrome or Edge on a Windows or Mac computer. If this page is inside another app or preview, open the website in its own browser tab. Some managed work computers restrict Bluetooth access.

About these faces

The subdials display clock values. Casio’s AMS marker is fixed to Amsterdam. Always-on screens use a simpler, darker display. Check the installed result on your own watch; battery life has not been measured.

Your computer connects directly to the watch. Installation history and device-test records are stored locally in your browser.
